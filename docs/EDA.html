EDA: data for a custom LLM4VKG matcher¶

  • CMT structured: Conference management data covering papers, authors, reviews, and program committees.
  • SIGKDD renamed: Conference organization data covering papers, participants, committees, sponsors, awards, and registration.
  • Chinook: A digital music store database containing artists, albums, tracks, customers, and sales.
  • Northwind: A trading company database containing products, suppliers, customers, orders, and shipping information.

1. Sources and import¶

Sources and setup: README. We analyze all local records.

PostgreSQL executes each dump in a separate temporary database.

4. Ontologies and evaluation material¶

We count RDF triples, predicates used in triples, and declared properties separately. For example, in :acceptPaper rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty, the predicate is rdf:type and the declared property is acceptPaper. These counts need not be equal. The absence of explicit instances does not imply an absence of ontology relationships.

We analyze TTL; OWL/XML is preserved as a source and is not counted as another dataset. We do not perform OWL reasoning or prove equivalence of the two serializations. Resource nodes are distinct IRIs and blank nodes in subject or object positions; literals are counted separately. Label and comment counts cover classes and properties.

6. Conclusions and next experiments¶

The numerical findings below come from the same execution as the tables.

  • Verified 115 tables and 31,032 records across four databases.
  • 17 columns in non-empty tables contain only NULL; 60 tables are empty. These cases cannot provide value examples.
  • 58 SQL–SPARQL pairs are available for RODI. Results on two scenarios do not represent the entire benchmark.
